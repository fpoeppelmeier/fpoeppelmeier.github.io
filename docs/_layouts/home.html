Unraveling tipping dynamics of marine biogeochemistry from past climate transitions

How do abrupt shifts in ocean circulation affect the biological carbon pump? BGCTip combines new isotope proxies, paleo reconstructions and the Bern3D model to find out, and applies the results to future tipping scenarios.
